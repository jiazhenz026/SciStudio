[2m    |z| <= 2 : 40 samples   2 < |z| <= 3 : 4 samples (S10 +2.1, S22 +2.2, S31 -2.1, S42 +2.4)[0m
[2m    |z| > 3  : 2 samples (S13 +99.2, S27 -13.7)   missing: 2[0m

3 sigma only removes the catastrophic failures. But look at the band between 2
and 3: four samples sitting 175-210 AU off the plate centre. On a 48-sample
viability plate that pattern is usually edge drift or a pipetting wobble, not
biology.

The trade is real and it is yours to make: tighten to 2 sigma and the summary
statistics get cleaner but you lose four samples of statistical power; stay at 3
and you keep the n but the suspect wells pull on every mean. For this assay I
would tighten it.

Select the QC node and change sigma_threshold from 3 to 2, then press Run.
You should keep 40 of 48.
