# Cancer vs stroma: triple-negative tumors

In both triple-negative tumors (CID44971 and CID4465), the invasive cancer
expresses more MMP9, CXCL10, CXCL9 and KRT16 than the stroma, and less COL14A1,
TNXB and AQP1. The breast epithelial genes that marked the cancer in the ER
tumors (GATA3, KRT18) do not move together here.
