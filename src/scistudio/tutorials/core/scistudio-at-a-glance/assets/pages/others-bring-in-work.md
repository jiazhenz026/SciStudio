# Bring in your work

You have analysis that predates SciStudio. **Bring in my work** — always in
the toolbar whenever a project is open — carries it in.

It asks a few questions, one at a time, about what you have and where it
should land, and sets up the project around your existing scripts and data.

If it was offered after level 4 and you skipped it — this is where it lives.
It is never locked behind anything.
