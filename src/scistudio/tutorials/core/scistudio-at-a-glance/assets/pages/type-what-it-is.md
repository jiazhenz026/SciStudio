# Data type

A data type says what may travel down a wire. Ports carry types, colours
show them, and only compatible ports connect — which is why a wrong wiring
fails when you draw it, not twenty minutes into a run.

You made a type of your own in level 2 (Image), gave it a colour, and used
it across two levels. A type you declare is a first-class citizen: previewers
can target it, blocks can require it, and the canvas shows it in your colour.
