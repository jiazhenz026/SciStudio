# Block

A block is SciStudio's basic unit of data processing: typed inputs on one
side, one job in the middle, typed outputs on the other.

You have handled blocks every way there is — dragged built-in ones in level 1,
written your own in levels 1 and 2, and watched the AI write one in level 4.

A block only ever sees what arrives on its inputs, and only hands on what it
puts on its outputs. That contract is what makes a graph of them composable —
and reproducible.
