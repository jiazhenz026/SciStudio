# Tips

- **Git stores the recipe, not the results.** Switching to an old branch
  brings back the workflow, not its outputs — press Run to regenerate. You
  met this in level 3.
- **Two histories, two jobs**: git tracks how your files changed; History
  tracks what ran, with what, and what came of it.
- **Export methods** — in the History panel — writes a methods description
  from a run's actual record. If you write papers, this may be the most
  useful button on the card.
- No git on your machine? Runs proceed as normal — they just have no version
  to restore to.
