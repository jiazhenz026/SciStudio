# Tips

- **One block, one job.** A block that does one thing is easier to test,
  easier to reuse, and easier to hand to the AI to improve. Splitting pays
  off because a good small block has somewhere to go — see the **My library**
  card.
- **Interactive blocks** pause the run and ask you — DataRouter and
  PairEditor already work this way, and your own blocks can too.
- **You do not have to write blocks yourself.** Describe the step to the AI
  and review what it writes; that was the whole of level 4.
