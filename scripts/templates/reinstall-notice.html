What's new

  • MiniApps: small interactive apps that open next to your workflow, such as a questionnaire that collects your input during a run
  • SciStudio now runs in your own AI apps, like ChatGPT. To try it, switch SciStudio to background mode and paste its link into the browser inside the ChatGPT desktop app.
  • Two new tutorials in the Learning Center: working with AI, and a multimodal analysis of real breast-cancer data
